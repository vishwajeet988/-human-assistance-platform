# Product Plan

## Vision

When you cannot be there, someone trustworthy can. The platform provides reliable, non-medical human assistance for appointments, elders, pets, and everyday errands, with transparent status, verified providers, family visibility, and safety operations.

## Primary users

- Customer: books and pays for assistance for themselves or someone they care for.
- Provider: a screened care partner who delivers non-medical assistance.
- Admin/support: operates bookings, verification, safety, payments, and customer support.

## MVP journeys

1. Customer registers, verifies contact information, adds a dependent/address, selects a service, gets a transparent estimate, pays in sandbox, and tracks the booking.
2. Provider registers, submits profile and documents, becomes eligible only after approval, receives a request, accepts it, updates service status, and completes the service.
3. Family member views a shared booking timeline and safety-relevant updates without receiving unnecessary private data.
4. Operations staff reviews verification, assigns/reassigns providers, handles cancellations/refunds, and responds to incidents.

## MVP scope

Service catalog, customer and provider onboarding, verification workflow, availability, pricing estimate, booking state machine, matching, sandbox payments, notifications, timeline, reviews, emergency/incident records, and an admin operations surface.

## Explicit boundaries

The MVP is non-medical. Providers cannot perform clinical procedures, diagnose, administer care beyond configured policy, or represent the platform as an emergency medical service. Real KYC, background-check, maps, SMS, WhatsApp, and payment production integrations are adapters and must be visibly marked as sandbox/development until configured.

## Post-MVP

Live maps and route ETA, real KYC/background checks, provider payouts, recurring care plans, multi-party permissions, richer family collaboration, advanced fraud/risk scoring, native mobile apps, and geographic expansion.
